Product Requirement Document (PRD)
Badminton Tournament Organizer & Live Scoring Platform
Version: 1.0 Status: Draft Prepared For: Product / Engineering / UI Team

1. Executive Summary
Product Name
SmashArena (Working Title)
Purpose
Build a web-based badminton tournament management platform that enables organizers to:
	•	Create and manage tournaments
	•	Organize events (Singles/Doubles/Team)
	•	Register participants
	•	Generate fixtures automatically
	•	Manage live scoring
	•	Publish results publicly
	•	Track rankings and statistics
	•	Improve tournament operations
The platform should support both:
Individual Tournament Model
Example:
	•	Men’s Singles
	•	Women’s Singles
	•	Men’s Doubles
	•	Women’s Doubles
	•	Mixed Doubles
Team / Franchise Tournament Model
Example:
	•	PBL style
	•	Team vs Team
	•	Multiple matches under one tie

2. Business Goals
Primary Goals
	•	Digitize badminton tournaments
	•	Eliminate manual score sheets
	•	Improve audience engagement
	•	Reduce tournament management effort
